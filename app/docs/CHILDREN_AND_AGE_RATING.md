# Children, Target Audience, and Age Rating

These declarations are made per app, not once for the entire developer account.

## Decision (Product Owner, 2026-10-03)

BEZY V1 is **not** designated for children. Google Play target audience: select
only age groups 13+ (do not select any group under 13). Apple: do not select
“Made for Kids”; choose “Not Applicable” under Age Categories. Store copy and
screenshots must stay consistent with a general-audience puzzle game.

## Background: recommended V1 position

Unless BEZY is intentionally marketed primarily to children and the product
owner specifically wants placement in Apple's Kids category, publish V1 as a
general-audience Puzzle game and answer the age-rating questionnaire honestly.
Do not select “Made for Kids” merely because the game is educational or safe
for children.

This recommendation avoids making a permanent Kids-category commitment while
still allowing families to download an age-appropriate game. Store copy and
screenshots must remain consistent with the declared audience.

## Apple

- The **Made for Kids** choice belongs to the individual app record.
- The developer account itself is not converted into a children-only account.
- If selected, choose the actual age band for which the product was designed.
- Once Apple approves an app as Made for Kids, that selection cannot be changed;
  later updates must continue following the Kids category rules.
- If not selecting Made for Kids, choose **Not Applicable** under Age Categories
  and Override, then complete the normal content questionnaire.

## Google Play

- **Target audience and content** is also completed separately for each app.
- Select only age groups the app was genuinely designed for.
- Selecting any child age group makes that app subject to Google Play Families
  Policy requirements.
- BEZY currently has no ads, accounts, tracking, purchases, communication, or
  transmitted personal data, which simplifies compliance but does not remove
  the requirement to answer accurately.

Final audience selection is a product/legal decision. Revisit this document if
advertising, analytics, accounts, social features, or purchases are introduced.
