# BEZY Privacy Policy

Effective date: 26 September 2026

BEZY is an offline puzzle game created by Ezy Levy. This policy explains how
the application handles information.

## Information collection

BEZY does not require an account and does not collect, transmit, sell, or share
personal information. The application does not contain advertising, analytics,
tracking technologies, social features, or in-app purchases.

## Information stored on the device

Game progress and preferences, such as completed stages, stars, sound, haptic
feedback, language, and dismissed instructional messages, are stored only on
the user's device. This information is not sent to the developer or to third
parties. It can be removed by clearing the application's storage or uninstalling
the application.

## Children's privacy

BEZY does not knowingly collect personal information from children or other
users. No user-generated content or communication service is provided.

## Changes

If the application's data practices change, this policy and the relevant store
privacy declarations will be updated before the changed version is released.
