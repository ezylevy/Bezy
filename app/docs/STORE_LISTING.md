# BEZY Store Listing Copy

The copy below intentionally describes only features visible in V1. Free Play
must not appear in store text or screenshots while its feature flag is disabled.

## English

**App name:** BEZY

**Subtitle (Apple):** Math paths and clever puzzles

**Short description (Google Play):** Build number paths, master special tiles, and solve 50 clever stages.

**Full description:**

BEZY is an offline path-building puzzle adventure that turns numbers into a
journey. Start at the edge of each board, choose your route, collect the right
values, and reach the center with the exact target.

Progress along an illustrated map of 50 handcrafted stages, unlocking each
stage by completing the one before it. New mechanics are introduced gradually,
including Jokers, teleporting doors, ice, mirrors, cloning, zero tiles, and
other strategic surprises.

- 50 increasingly challenging stages
- Hebrew and English interface
- One continuous journey: a stage map with linear progression
- Works completely offline
- No account, advertisements, tracking, or in-app purchases
- Progress and settings remain on your device

Plan carefully, experiment with routes, and find your way to the exact number.

**Keywords (Apple):** puzzle,math,numbers,path,logic,brain,offline,education

**Primary category:** Games — Puzzle

**Secondary category:** Education

## Review notes

BEZY is fully available without login or network access. Start from “Journey”
and open the next unlocked stage on the map; stages unlock one after another. No review
account, special hardware, purchases, or external service is required.
