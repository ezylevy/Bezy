BEZY Privacy Policy

Effective date: 28 September 2026

BEZY is an offline puzzle game created by Ezy Levy.

Information collection

BEZY does not require an account and does not collect, transmit, sell, or share personal information. The application contains no advertising, analytics, tracking technologies, social features, or in-app purchases.

Information stored on the device

Game progress and preferences—including completed stages, stars, sound, haptic feedback, language, and dismissed instructions—are stored only on the user's device. They are not sent to the developer or third parties. Users can remove them by clearing application storage or uninstalling BEZY.

Children's privacy

BEZY does not knowingly collect personal information from children or other users. It provides no user-generated content or communication service.

Changes

If BEZY's data practices change, this policy and the store privacy declarations will be updated before the changed version is released.
